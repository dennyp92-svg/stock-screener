Privacy Policy - Stock Scanner Pro

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Stock Scanner Pro ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or share personal information from users. Stock market data displayed in the App is sourced from third-party financial data providers (Yahoo Finance and Financial Modeling Prep) and is publicly available market information.

Watchlist Data

Any stocks you add to your personal watchlist are stored locally and are not shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App uses AI analysis powered by Anthropic's Claude API to generate stock insights. No personal user data is sent to this service — only publicly available stock market data.

Disclaimer

This App is for informational and educational purposes only. It does not constitute financial advice. Always consult a licensed financial advisor before making investment decisions.
